## Brief 1 (Brand) — why this shape
The strategy layer had to pick a gap the research says is real but unoccupied. The strongest such gap is trust: every competitor competes on taste, origin, or function while the documented consumer anxiety is about *processing honesty*. A "batch record brand" is the only positioning in the research that converts the category's central paradox (freshness vs industrialization) directly into an asset instead of a secret. Confidence: high on the gap (explicitly researched); medium on the name (trademark screen outstanding, declared). Uncertainty marker: the mood-board pins are cited by ID because the board lives in Assignment 1, which was not shipped as a file here — coherence lines therefore anchor primarily on findings/insights, which *are* fully present in the research file.

## Brief 2 (Product/Packaging) — why this shape
Two research facts steer everything: glass is the fastest-growing premium format tier, and visual signifiers have stopped working on the shelf. The combination points at a perishable unpasteurized bottle whose standout element is informational (the Batch Panel), not decorative — differentiation typed against all five researched competitor archetypes so "different" is describable, per the brief's explicit wording ("not just better"). The can-vs-glass channel tension is handled openly: bottle now, can Phase 2 contingent on cold-chain economics, exactly as the research cautions.

## Brief 3 (Campaign) — why this shape
The research traces advertising's escalation from serene lifestyle to kinetic performance; the quiet documentary lane is therefore the differentiated register, and it happens to be the only register compatible with a promise built on receipts — you cannot hype your way out of a forensic brand. Production path is deliberately hybrid rather than purely AI: claim-bearing pixels must match real batch records, so the product itself is photographed and AI is fenced to exploration/motionization/localization behind four named human gates. That fence is what makes the AI path "genuinely thought-through" per the evaluation criteria rather than a cost excuse.
