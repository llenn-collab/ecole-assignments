# LAYER_A — readable audit (plain English first, technical appendix last)

## Executive reading
The Assignment 3 deck for Off Hours is built as one continuous story: the brand and audience from
Assignment 1, the voice and content system from Assignment 2, then new search and earned-media work.
The strategy's centre of gravity is a participatory public archive of real evening wind-down notes
("The Landing Hour — India's 10 PM Archive"), because the evidence package's strongest signal points
at cooperative, visible, community-shaped campaigns rather than solo brand announcements. Everything
loud is sequenced after everything quiet: long-tail search and embargoed editor briefings first, the
public opening second, slow grounded amplification third. Numbers are never invented: the keyword
priorities rest on intent and relevance, the media list on verified real outlets, and the business
arithmetic on Assignment 1's own stated targets.

## Walkthrough of significant findings
- **The cooperation signal.** The package's densest supportive cluster sits in the visibility sector
  (partnership deity, life gate, environment-feeds-action, "favours cooperative affairs"). Plain read:
  the campaign should be something people join, not something they watch. Technical basis: OPERATOR
  row C005/H1/A001 (package evidence E169–E191).
- **The execution gate.** The package marks the execution channel as obstruction-shaped and favours
  quiet planning over open-front pushes. Plain read: pre-launch stays gated and long-tail; the loud
  moment is handed to partners and earned coverage. Technical basis: OPERATOR row C006/H2.
- **The exposure guard.** Deception-exposure markers align independently with the brief's own
  prohibition on invented data. Plain read: honesty is both a rule and a strategy; the label-answers
  content is the brand's strongest searchable asset. Technical basis: OPERATOR row C007 + REQ-022.
- **The duplication trap.** A centre-palace "documents pile up" marker reads as a consolidation
  directive. Plain read: one page per keyword group, one message hierarchy; the site never competes
  with itself. Technical basis: OPERATOR row C010/H4.
- **The timing lattice, de-mystified.** Phase guidance was translated into execution order (quiet →
  visible → grounded), never into calendar predictions. Technical basis: OPERATOR row C012/AX-002,
  labelled assumption-indexed upstream.

## Candidates and selection
| Candidate | Shape | Why selected / rejected |
|---|---|---|
| The Landing Hour archive (participatory, community-hosted) | Selected | Cooperation signal + no fabricated data needed + continuity with A2 community mechanics |
| Data-report-led campaign | Runner-up, reconciled | Would require real survey data the brief forbids inventing; kept as the expert Landing Protocol document asset |
| Stealth long-tail only | Rejected as standalone | Correct for pre-launch and keywords, but cannot carry the earned-attention objective alone |
| Paid-awareness burst | Vetoed | Contradicts A1's "paid amplifies, never leads" and the package's frontal-attack penalty path |
Margin note: selection margin is qualitative but stable — the archive is the only candidate satisfying
the cooperation signal, the no-fabrication constraint and the A2 community continuity simultaneously.

## Confidence and residual risk
Confidence band: high on structure (requirements coverage, lint, ledger closure), medium on the
marketing translation layer (interpretive), low-by-design on anything numeric (nothing invented).
What would change the plan: real keyword data from free tools contradicting the intent-based
priorities; editor first-looks declining (then the archive opens community-first); A2 restatement
being superseded by a corrected source file from the instructor.
