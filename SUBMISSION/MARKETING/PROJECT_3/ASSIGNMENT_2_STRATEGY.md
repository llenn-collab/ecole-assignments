## Slide 1 — Content Marketing Objective

### What content must achieve for this brand

> **Content must make the brand the place the audience goes to learn the one thing that is actually holding them back — plainly, in one voice, without shouting.**

### The two primary objectives (choose 1–2)

**1. Build awareness — through display, not through volume**
**[CHART: C003, E085, E086, E091, E093, E095]**
One palace carries the display gate (景门), the widest-reaching spirit (九天) and the radiance star (天英) — and that palace's gate *feeds* its house (门生宫), with a stem pair whose own reading is that cooperation goes smoothly. Awareness is available here; it arrives as something *shown well*, and it multiplies when it is made *with* someone.

**2. Educate consumers**
**[CHART: C002, E005, E006, E012, E022]**
The chart's governing spirit (值符) is 天辅, the *assisting/teaching* star, and the duty gate (值使) is 杜门, the gate of closing and going inside. The two instruments that define the moment are therefore "teaching" and "depth". Education is not a content type for this brand; it is the mode of authority it can occupy.

### Why these objectives and not others

**Why community is deliberately *not* a primary objective** — even though the alliance deity 六合 sits plainly in the chart:
**[CHART: C005, E023, E029, E031, E032]** The alliance palace also carries 丁加癸『朱雀投江』 — the classic marker of *messages that sink and do not arrive* — plus 宫迫门 (the house presses its gate) and 三奇受制 (the extraordinary capacity is locked). Partnership is structurally desirable; **announcement-style messaging through it is not**. Community stays in the plan as a *participant* objective (Slide 8), not as a headline objective.

**Why not "generate traffic" or "support conversion" as primaries:**
**[CHART: C004, E074, E075, E080, E082]** Value does accrue in this chart — but in the generation gate 生门 paired with the hidden-support deity 太阴, under a stem pair that explicitly favours the quiet party (利阴害阳). Those are *quiet* results, which a traffic objective would drown out. Conversion is tracked (Slide 11) rather than led with.

**Why these two objectives matter for this audience:**
The audience in this chart is reached by being *shown* something (display palace) and *kept* by being taught something (governing star). That is a person who saves content rather than commenting on it, who arrives curious and stays for the depth — which is exactly the persona behaviour to confirm against Assignment 1 `[SLOT]`.

## Slide 2 — Brand Story & Communication

### Core brand message — the one idea

> **"The door opens quietly."**
> *[CHART: C001, C013, E017, E018, E019, E020]*

The chart's keystone is the explicit marker 辛加丁『狱神得奇』 — *"the prison-god meets the star-maiden: the confined gain release, and gain is doubled"* — sitting with 玉女守门 (the Jade Maiden guarding the gate: hidden patronage) and 宫生门 (the house feeds the gate). The same palace carries the sprouting stage (长生) beside an exhausted one (绝): **the release is real, and it has to be coaxed out of a depleted substrate.** That is the brand idea: not a breakthrough, an *opening*. Not louder, *clearer*.

**[SLOT]** *(brand name)* — the one that says the quiet thing plainly.

### Tone of voice — four characteristics

| Characteristic | What it sounds like | Chart basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Instructive** | it teaches before it sells; it assumes you are smart and busy | **[CHART: C002, E022]** 天辅 as governing star |
| **Restrained** | never loud, never urgent, never a countdown | **[CHART: C002, E012]** 杜门 as duty gate |
| **Plain** | short sentences, no jargon, no superlatives | **[CHART: C005, E029]** 朱雀投江: pushed and ornate messages sink and never arrive |
| **Quietly warm** | as if to one person, not an audience | **[CHART: C004, E074, E019]** 太阴 hidden support · 玉女守门 hidden patronage |

### Communication principles — what it should always / never feel like

**Always:** simple · useful · calm · specific · one idea per piece · crediting the reader's intelligence
**Never:** preachy · alarmist · technical-for-effect · judgmental · competitively sneering · urgency-manufacturing

**[CHART: C006, E049, E050, E055, E057, E063, E064, E069, E071]** The never-list is not a matter of taste here. The chart's three friction palaces hold 惊门 (the alarm gate) with 玄武 (opacity that misleads) and 天柱 (the breaking word); 伤门 (the wound gate) with 腾蛇 (entanglement and repetition); and 死门 (the death gate) with 九地 and 复见螣蛇 (the same error repeated). Alarm, opacity, argument and repetition are the four registers the structure marks as *damaging to whoever forces them*.

### Sample copy — five lines demonstrating the register

1. "You don't need more willpower. You need the thing you already do to work."
2. "Here's the label, read plainly. Three lines matter — we'll show you which."
3. "No countdown. It will still be here tomorrow."
4. "We fixed the boring part. That's the whole announcement."
5. "If you read one line: the 4pm slump is a food problem, not a character problem."

*(Line 5 uses the brief's own snack example `[SOURCE_EXAMPLE]`; swap in your brand's version of "the thing people blame themselves for".)*

**On the Gymshark tone-of-voice discussion:** the register used there — bold, energetic, community-forward, high tempo — is a *different pole* from what this structure supports. If your Assignment 1 brand was positioned that way, this is the strategic tension to resolve explicitly in your deck rather than smoothing over. **[ASSUMPTION A-001: subject binding — declared, medium risk]**

| 3 | **Quiet Proof** | Trust | Real evidence, unedited, in the customer's own words — no before/after theatre | **[CHART: C004, E074, E075, E084]** 生门 + 太阴 + 天任, gate feeds house |
| 4 | **Inside the Room** | Belonging / depth | Most brands give you the summary; this one gives you the long version | **[CHART: C002, E012, E006]** 杜门 as the duty gate: closing, going deeper inside |
| 5 | **Standards & Refusals** | Differentiation | A brand that visibly refuses things is much easier to trust | **[CHART: C006, E049, E050, E055, E057, E063, E064]** the friction markers say *what is refused* |

### Why these are not generic buckets

Each pillar names a **function that the chart actually distinguishes**, not a vibe:

- Pillar 1 is not "educational" in the generic sense — it is *the governing star's office*, which makes it the brand's source of authority rather than a content category.
- Pillar 2 is display **as a service to the audience** (景门 = screen, view, document), not as self-promotion; the palace's gate feeds its house, meaning the showing has to give something back.
- Pillar 3 is proof delivered the way the chart delivers it: through 太阴, the hidden channel — testimonials that stay in the customer's voice, not polished into ad copy.
- Pillar 4 turns the chart's *most restrictive* element (杜门, the duty gate) into an asset: depth is the thing the door keeps behind it.
- Pillar 5 is the inversion that makes the strategy distinctive: the chart spends three palaces naming what goes wrong when you force things — so the brand publishes its refusals.

**Pillar rotation rule:** Pillars 1 and 3 carry trust; 2 carries reach; 4 carries belonging; 5 carries differentiation. Every piece of content is assigned to exactly one pillar, so the feed never becomes a bucket of mixed intent. **[ASSUMPTION A-003: content-function mapping, medium risk]**

## Slide 7 — Hero Content / Campaign Idea

**Route: Insight → Big Idea → Key Message → Execution**

### Insight
**[CHART: C005, C013, E029, E014, E015]** The audience does not lack options. They are *drowning in noise* — and in this chart the noise marker is explicit: 朱雀投江, *the message sinks into the river and never arrives*. Meanwhile the keystone says something quieter: what is confined gains release, and it is guaranteed by hidden patronage — **but it has to be coaxed out of a depleted substrate.** So the honest insight is: *people do not need more energy; they need one thing that works when they have none.*

### Big Idea
> ## **The Quiet Unlock**
> *A campaign about opening one door at a time — a brand that explains the thing plainly, refuses to shout it, and lets the result be quiet.*

### Key message
> **"You don't need to try harder. You need one thing that actually works — and someone to explain it plainly."**

### Execution — a four-part arc
**[CHART: C001, C002, C003, C004, C006]**

| Beat | What happens | Pillar | Chart basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Teach** | Label literacy series: what matters, what doesn't, in plain language | Plain Teach | 天辅 as governing star |
| **2. Prove** | Customers' own words, unedited — including the awkward ones | Quiet Proof | 太阴 + 生门 |
| **3. Refuse** | Publish what the brand will not do (no countdowns, no fabricated urgency, no shortcuts) | Standards & Refusals | the three friction palaces |

**Note on scope:** this is deliberately an *individual* hero idea, not a fully integrated campaign — per the brief, the objective is to demonstrate the move from consumer insight to creative idea. **[ASSUMPTION A-007: illustration uses the brief's own example, low risk]**

## Slide 11 — Measurement & Success

### Metrics, each tied to an objective

| Objective | KPI | Chart basis | What "healthy" looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness through display | **Reach** | **[CHART: E086, E085]** 景门 + 九天 | rising, without a rise in argument threads |
| Education | **Saves and returns to a piece** | **[CHART: E005, E022, E075]** 天辅 + 生门 | education pieces are the most-saved |
| Trust / quiet proof | **Private interactions** — saves, DMs, follows-from-post | **[CHART: E074]** 太阴 | private response ≥ public response |
| Participation | **Conversations continued** (threads answered, questions asked) — *not comment volume* | **[CHART: E024]** 休门 | questions, not arguments |
| Entry | **Link clicks / CTR** | **[CHART: E097]** 开门 | steady, secondary |
| Alliance | **Co-created pieces and partner mentions** | **[CHART: E023]** 六合 | grows without a broadcast push |
| **Stop-signal** | **Argument threads and complaints per cycle** | **[CHART: E050, E064, E069]** 惊门 / 伤门 / 腾蛇 | **flat or falling** — this is the negative indicator |

Seven metrics, each mapped to an objective, one of which is a **negative** indicator. A framework that cannot fail is not a framework.

### After one month — how you would know whether the strategy is working

> **It is working** if the *saves and returns* are the rising line, the private responses are at least as strong as the public ones, the anchor pieces are the ones being kept — and the argument threads are flat. That is the chart's own test: quiet accumulation, in the palace that generates value, with the noise register staying quiet.
>
> **It is not working** if reach is rising while saves stay flat and the comment threads have turned into arguments. That would mean the brand has drifted into the friction register — and the fix is not more volume, it is a smaller claim, said plainer.
>
> **[CHART: C004, C005, C006 · E074, E075, E029, E050, E064]**

### What would falsify this strategy

Stated deliberately, because a strategy that cannot be wrong cannot be graded:
- If private interactions never exceed public ones over a full cycle, **C004 (value accrues quietly)** is not holding for this brand.
- If a low-key partnership message outperforms a broadcast push in the same channel, **C005 (announcement through the alliance channel fails)** is not holding.
- If a contest/duet format produces the brand's best trust results, **C006 (friction registers)** is not holding.
- If depth pieces underperform short display pieces on *trust* measures while winning on reach, **C002/C003** need re-weighting.
