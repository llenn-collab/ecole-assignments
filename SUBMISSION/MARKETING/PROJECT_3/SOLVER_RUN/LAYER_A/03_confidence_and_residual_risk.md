## Residual risks carried forward, not closed

1. **Slow-channel misread** — a favourable but low-force channel will look weak in month one. Mitigation is
   in the measurement framework (judge across a full cycle) and named in the annotated risk table.
2. **Inaudibility risk** — restraint applied everywhere would produce a brand nobody hears. The display
   surface and the reach metric exist to hold this open.
3. **Partnership-channel failure** — announcements pushed through alliance channels under-deliver. Mitigated
   by keeping those channels two-way; the failure mode is named in the deck rather than only in the notes.
4. **Creator substitution** — if either recommended creator is conflicted or geographically mismatched, the
   four filters reproduce the recommendation. The names are illustrative of the filters, never the reverse.
5. **Attribution fog in month one** — content-attributed trial starts will be partly guessed. The framework
   reports source mix rather than a single number.
6. **Declined challenger lane** — a more confrontational posture is available in the evidence and was
   deliberately not taken. It remains available if leadership prefers it; the cost is the calm register.

## What would falsify the strategy

- **Saves and returns never lead**: trust content fails to out-save reach content across a full cycle.
- **Private interactions never exceed public ones** across a full cycle.
- **Depth films underperform short films on trial starts** (not on views).
- **Reach stays flat while the calendar runs.** Then the mix — not the voice — is wrong.
- **Argument threads rise** despite the refusal list. Then restraint is being applied to the wrong variable.
