# LAYER A · 00 — Executive reading

**One page. What was decided and why.**

## The decision

Off Hours should run **"Quiet Authority"**: one teaching voice, three surfaces chosen by audience behaviour,
and a hero campaign built inside the brand's own ten-night judgement window.

## Why this one

Four candidate strategies were assembled from the evidence and scored against the assignment's requirements
and Assignment 1's constraints. Two were viable; Two were vetoed outright.

| Candidate | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Quiet Authority** | **Selected.** Full requirement coverage, no constraint conflicts |
| Education-First, Newsletter-Led | Viable runner-up. Fully compliant but leaves the display surface idle |
| Advocacy Blitz | **Vetoed** — conflicts with Assignment 1's named non-priorities |
| Broadcast Reach Sprint | **Vetoed** — conflicts with Assignment 1's named non-priorities |

The selected strategy leads on **requirement coverage (1.00 vs 0.77)** and on hard-constraint compliance; the
margin over the runner-up is narrow (0.84 vs 0.82), which is stated rather than hidden.

## The three load-bearing findings

1. **Authority and restraint sit in the same place in this brand's evidence.** That is unusual — most brands
   must trade one for the other. It is the single most exploitable fact in the analysis, and it is why the
   strategy concentrates rather than diversifies.
2. **Reaching and earning trust behave differently here.** They get separate surfaces, separate content
   formats and separate metrics. Averaging them would hide both.
3. **The plan is a loop, not a stack.** Nine traces connect the plan's surfaces into a graph in which nothing
   is a dead end. The referral link is what closes it.

## The named risks

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| The quiet channel is favourable but slow; month-one judgment would misread it | Judge across a full cycle; the annotated table says so explicitly |
| Restraint can become inaudibility | Display content carries reach by design; reach is a tracked metric |
| Partnership announcements under-deliver in alliance channels | Community channels stay two-way; no announcements routed through them |
| Creator conflicts and geography unverified | Three pre-activation checks; the *filters*, not the names, are the deliverable |
| A challenger posture is available and deliberately declined | Recorded as a decision with its own cost, not as an omission |

## Confidence

Objective selection, voice, pillars and refusals: **high**. Surface roles and cadence: **medium**.
Creator recommendations: **medium** (filters strong, verification open). Metric thresholds: **medium**.
