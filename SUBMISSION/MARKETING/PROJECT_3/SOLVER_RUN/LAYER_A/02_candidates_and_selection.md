# LAYER A · 02 — Candidates and selection

## The four candidates

**S-01 · Quiet Authority — SELECTED.** One teaching voice. Display carries reach, depth carries trust, the
private channel carries accumulation. Deliberate cadence. A small set of practical educators as the creator
programme. *Final 0.8407 · fit 0.9800 · coverage 1.000 · no violations.*

**S-04 · Education-First, Newsletter-Led — VIABLE RUNNER-UP.** Maximum depth; an owned publication carries the
strategy; light display. Fully compliant, but under-uses the display and discovery capacity Assignment 1
already operates. *Final 0.8163 · fit 0.8691 · coverage 0.773 · no violations.*

**S-02 · Broadcast Reach Sprint — VETOED.** Launch-week awareness burst, mass display, high frequency,
celebrity amplification. Vetoed by Assignment 1's named non-priorities and its deliberate phasing.
*Final 0.3778 · compliance multiplier 0.0.*

**S-03 · Advocacy Blitz — VETOED.** Competitive user challenges, duets and callouts, macro creators driving
participation. Vetoed by Assignment 1's named non-priorities and its strategic stance.
*Final 0.4278 · compliance multiplier 0.0.*

## How the scores were built

Five weighted components, with hard-constraint compliance applied as a **multiplier** rather than folded into
the average — a candidate that breaks a constraint cannot be rescued by strength elsewhere:

| Component | Weight | S-01 | S-04 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grounding | 0.30 | 0.6 (computed) | 1.0 (explicit) |
| Corroboration (capped at 9) | 0.20 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| Contradiction penalty | 0.15 | 0.778 | 0.778 |
| Board consistency | 0.05 | pass | pass |
| Requirement fit | 0.30 | 0.980 | 0.869 |
| **Hard-constraint multiplier** | × | **1.0** | **1.0** |

S-01 wins despite a lower grounding tier because its requirement coverage is complete while S-04's display
gap makes four must-level requirements partial. This is the rubric working as intended: coverage of what the
brief asked for outranks the tidiness of a single evidence tier.

## The rejected candidates and why they matter

Both vetoed candidates are *attractive* — that is why they were assembled. S-02 is what a growth team would
reach for first; S-03 is what a community team would. They are recorded with their rejection reasons so the
decision is visible as a decision, and so a future cohort can argue with it on the record.

## Residual deliberation

The 0.024 margin between S-01 and S-04 is narrow enough to record honestly. If the brand concludes that
depth alone should carry this cycle — for example, if the team cannot produce two long-form anchors a month —
**S-04 is a legitimate alternative**, and the change is a substitution, not a failure.
