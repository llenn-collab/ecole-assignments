# Sample copy pack — Off Hours

Fifteen lines across the six concepts, written to demonstrate the tone of voice in P2-02. Every line is
**calm, plain-spoken, evidence-led, quietly warm** — and every line refuses a device from the never-list
(no countdowns, no streaks, no fear, no clinical claims).

## Concept 1 — "What magnesium glycinate actually does" (carousel)

| Slide | Copy |
|---|---|
| 1 | **What magnesium glycinate actually does.** *Forty seconds. No jargon. No promises.* |
| 2 | Magnesium is a mineral your body uses in hundreds of reactions — including the ones that let muscles and nerves settle. |
| 3 | Glycinate is the form chosen for tolerance, not for strength. It is gentler on the stomach than some cheaper forms. |
| 4 | What it will not do: sedate you. Nothing in this sachet is a sleeping pill, and we would tell you if it were. |
| 5 | **A landing, not a pill.** Read the whole list on the pack — we print it plainly. |

## Concept 2 — "We read our own label out loud" (long-form)

> **Opening line:** "This is the back of our own pack. We're going to read it in full, including the two lines most brands put in small print."

> **Closing line:** "If any of that sounded less impressive than you expected — good. That's what honest looks like at this price."

## Concept 3 — "Six things we refuse to say about our own product" (carousel of 6)

| Slide | Copy |
|---|---|
| 1 | **What we won't say about our own product.** *Six claims we refuse to make — and why.* |
| 2 | **"Fall asleep in minutes."** We won't say it. This is a wind-down ritual, not a sedative — and anyone promising minutes is selling you something else. |
| 3 | **"Clinically proven to cure insomnia."** We won't say it. Insomnia is a clinical condition and deserves a clinician, not a sachet. |
| 4 | **"You'll never need anything else."** We won't say it. If your evenings are still not landing after ten nights, the answer might not be a drink. |
| 5 | **"Trusted by thousands."** We won't say it until it is true at a scale we can show — and when it is, we will show the count, not the word. |
| 6 | **"The secret to perfect sleep."** We won't say it. There is no secret, and a brand that sells you one is selling you a second disappointment. |

## Concept 4 — "Night 1 of 10" (Reel — on-screen text only)

| Time | On-screen copy |
|---|---|
| 0–15s | *(no text — the shot carries it)* |
| 20–25s | **NIGHT 1 OF 10.** |

## Concept 5 — "The shelf" (carousel / private note)

> **Intro:** "Every note below is a customer's own sentence. We have not fixed the grammar, and we have not removed the two that are lukewarm. That is the shelf."

> **Private-channel version:** "This week on the shelf: a night that worked, a night that didn't, and someone who skipped two nights and came back. Bring yours on Friday."

## Concept 6 — "Ask us about the label" (Stories → video)

> **Prompt:** "Ask us anything about the label. Especially the awkward ones. We answer every question on camera — including the ones we don't enjoy."

## Channel variants of one idea ("Night 1 of 10")

| Surface | Line |
|---|---|
| Instagram caption (short) | **Night 1 of 10.** Laptop closed. Lights down. One glass. We're not asking you to sleep earlier — just to land better. |
| YouTube description (long) | What actually happens in the twenty minutes after you close your laptop — and why the habit of not switching off is beatable. Tonight is night one of ten. No countdown, no streaks; miss a night if you need to. |
| WhatsApp / e-mail (plain text) | **Night 1 of 10.** Which means tonight's only task is this: what time did your evening actually end? Reply if you like. No need to. |

## The refusals (used across every surface)

1. We will not claim a sleep outcome.
2. We will not use a countdown, a streak or a scarcity timer.
3. We will not describe a customer's experience in our own words.
4. We will not shout in an emergency voice, because there is no emergency.
5. We will not tell you to sleep earlier. We will only help you land.
