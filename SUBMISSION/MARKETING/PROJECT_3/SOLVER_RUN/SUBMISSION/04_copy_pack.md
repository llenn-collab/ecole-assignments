# Sample copy pack. Off Hours

Fifteen lines across the six concepts, written to show the tone of voice in P2-02. Every line is calm, plain-spoken, evidence-led and quietly warm. Every line refuses a device from the never-list: no countdowns, no streaks, no fear, no clinical claims.

## Concept 1. "What magnesium glycinate does" (carousel)

| Slide | Copy |
|---|---|
| 1 | What magnesium glycinate does. Forty seconds. No jargon. No promises. |
| 2 | Magnesium is a mineral your body uses in hundreds of reactions, including the reactions which let muscles and nerves settle. |
| 3 | Glycinate is the form chosen for tolerance, not for strength. Your stomach handles glycinate more easily than some cheaper forms. |
| 4 | What magnesium will not do: sedate you. Nothing in this sachet is a sleeping pill, and we would tell you if one were. |
| 5 | A landing, not a pill. Read the whole list on the pack. We print the list plainly. |

## Concept 2. "We read our own label out loud" (long-form)

Opening line: "This is the back of our own pack. We will read the whole thing, including the two lines most brands put in small print."

Closing line: "If some of this sounded less impressive than you expected, good. Honest looks like this at this price."

## Concept 3. "Six things we refuse to say about our own product" (carousel of 6)

| Slide | Copy |
|---|---|
| 1 | What we will not say about our own product. Six claims we refuse to make, and why. |
| 2 | "Fall asleep in minutes." We will not say this. A wind-down ritual is not a sedative, and anyone promising minutes sells you something else. |
| 3 | "Clinically proven to cure insomnia." We will not say this. Insomnia is a clinical condition, and insomnia deserves a clinician, not a sachet. |
| 4 | "You will never need anything else." We will not say this. If your evenings still do not land after ten nights, the answer might not be a drink. |
| 5 | "Trusted by thousands." We will not say this until the count is true at a scale worth showing. When the count is true, we show the number, not the word. |
| 6 | "The secret to perfect sleep." We will not say this. No secret exists, and a brand selling you one sells you a second disappointment. |

## Concept 4. "Night 1 of 10" (Reel, on-screen text only)

| Time | On-screen copy |
|---|---|
| 0 to 15s | (no text, the shot carries it) |
| 20 to 25s | NIGHT 1 OF 10. |

## Concept 5. "The shelf" (carousel and private note)

Intro: "Every note below is a customer's own sentence. We left the grammar alone, and we left in the two lukewarm notes. This shelf is the honest one."

Private-channel version: "This week on the shelf: a night which worked, a night which did not, and someone who skipped two nights and came back. Bring your note on Friday."

## Concept 6. "Ask us about the label" (Stories to video)

Prompt: "Ask us anything about the label. The awkward questions first. We answer every question on camera, including the questions we do not enjoy."

## Channel variants of one idea ("Night 1 of 10")

| Surface | Line |
|---|---|
| Instagram caption (short) | Night 1 of 10. Laptop closed. Lights down. One glass. We are not asking you to sleep earlier, only to land better. |
| YouTube description (long) | What happens in the twenty minutes after you close your laptop, and why the habit of not switching off is beatable. Tonight is night one of ten. No countdown, no streaks. Miss a night if you need to. |
| WhatsApp and e-mail (plain text) | Night 1 of 10. Tonight's only task: what time did your evening end? Reply if you like. No need to. |

## The refusals (used across every surface)

1. We will not claim a sleep outcome.
2. We will not use a countdown, a streak or a scarcity timer.
3. We will not describe a customer's experience in our own words.
4. We will not shout in an emergency voice, because no emergency exists.
5. We will not tell you to sleep earlier. We will only help you land.
