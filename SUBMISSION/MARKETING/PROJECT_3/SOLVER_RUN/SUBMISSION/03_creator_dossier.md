# Creator dossier. Off Hours

Purpose: the two creator recommendations from the deck (P2-09), expanded to a working briefing sheet.
Selection basis: the four-filter fit test, applied before any reach consideration. Follower counts stay out of the selection process and out of every argument in this document.

---

## Filter results

| Filter | What passing looks like | Why the filter matters for Off Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Teaching ratio | Most content explains a mechanism. Little of the content reacts to trends. | We borrow a habit of explaining, not a posting frequency. |
| 2. Private-engagement ratio | Saves, questions and DM replies run out of proportion to likes. | The real signal from our audience is private. A creator who earns saves earns the right attention. |
| 3. Reply tone | Comment threads read as questions, not arguments. | Argument threads are the register which damages an ingested product fastest. |
| 4. Disclosure comfort | The creator will state on camera when a piece is a paid partnership. | The ingredient-sceptic punishes hidden sponsorship harder than price. |

## Creator A. Shabbir Ahmed, Sleep Psychologist

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Platforms | YouTube (Sleep Psychologist), practice site sleeppsychologist.in |
| Base | Navi Mumbai, India |
| Category | Applied sleep psychology. Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), ACT-I, mindfulness-based practice. |
| Credentials | MA Psychology (IGNOU). PhD scholar researching sleep psychology. Author of The Sleep Foundations, eight evidence-based practices grounded in sleep science and CBT-I. |
| Public position | Explicitly method-led: "science, compassion, and CBT-I, not sleep hacks". His published work names the problem of conflicting advice, quick fixes and unfounded supplement claims. |
| Audience | Adults with persistent sleep difficulty, burnout and stress. Professionals seeking behavioural answers rather than chemical ones. |
| Filter 1, teaching | Pass. His content is explainer-led by construction, and his own framing rejects hacks. |
| Filter 2, private engagement | Pass by construction, not by count. A practice-based educator builds a client and question pipeline, not a like pipeline. Verify current save and DM volume before contracting. |
| Filter 3, reply tone | Pass. Practitioner audience, so questions rather than pile-ons. |
| Filter 4, disclosure | Verify at contracting. Not publicly established either way. |

Why this person and why this brand. Off Hours is not a sleep treatment and will never claim to be. Shabbir Ahmed is the one kind of partner who holds this line and makes the brand more credible by holding it. He explains what a magnesium, L-theanine and chamomile evening drink does and does not do, in plain language, with the authority to say: this is a ritual, not a therapy. The brand needs a third party to draw this exact boundary, because a brand will never credibly disclaim its own category. His audience is the ingredient-sceptic who distrusts claims. Handed the explanation rather than a script, he reaches this audience as proof rather than as advertising.

Role in the strategy.
1. One long-form (6 to 8 min): what happens in the body during a wind-down, and where a night drink fits, and where the night drink does not fit.
2. One label review: he reads the Off Hours pack on camera, ingredient by ingredient, including what he would tell a client to expect.
3. One Q&A from his own audience's questions, answered without a product script.
4. He carries Plain Labels. Never used for a launch announcement.

Guard. No clinical or treatment claims, no efficacy promises, and no hint of substitution for CBT-I or medical care. If a brief needs such a claim, the partnership does not proceed.

## Creator B. Shruti Maheshwari, co-founder, Sleep Gurukul (Mumbai)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Platforms | Sleep Gurukul (LinkedIn and owned practice channels), founder-led presence |
| Base | Mumbai, Maharashtra, India |
| Category | Holistic sleep and recovery. Sleep coaching, breath-work, yoga, mental wellness. Individual and corporate programmes. |
| Credentials and recognitions | Co-founder of Sleep Gurukul. Harvard Medical School education listed on her professional profile. The practice earned recognition at the BW Wellbeing Festival in Mumbai for Excellence in Holistic Wellbeing and Sleep. Co-author of the practice's book Sleep, Your Forgotten Superpower. |
| Audience | Indian professionals, strongly female, metro-skewed 25 to 40. Interested in breath-work, routine and sustainable wellbeing rather than gadgets. |
| Filter 1, teaching | Pass. Coaching and practice-led content: routines, breathing, evening structure. |
| Filter 2, private engagement | Pass by construction. Coaching practices rest on one-to-one trust, and the public following is a by-product, not the product. |
| Filter 3, reply tone | Pass. Community and wellbeing discourse, so questions rather than contests. |
| Filter 4, disclosure | Verify at contracting. |

Why this person and why this brand. Off Hours sells the ending of a day. Sleep Gurukul teaches the ending of a day. Breath-work and a guided wind-down demonstrate "a landing, not a pill" more directly than any product claim, and a product alone will never show a practice. Her practice is India-native, built for Indian working rhythms rather than translated from Western sleep content, which matches the positioning of Off Hours for late-working Indian cities. Her corporate-wellness and clinic programmes form the natural on-ramp to the Phase 2 partnership plan, with a partner who already holds the buyer's trust.

Role in the strategy.
1. A guided ten-night wind-down series for the private channel. One breath practice per night, no product in frame.
2. The pre-roll practice for the "Night 1 of 10" film, credited and disclosed.
3. A co-hosted evening-practice session at the end of the first ten-night cohort.
4. The introduction path into Phase 2 corporate wellness programmes.
5. She carries The Landing.

Guard. No medical or therapeutic claims about the product. The practice content stands alone, and the product appears as the object of the ritual, never as the subject of the practice.

## What we are not doing

- No celebrity or macro-creator blitz. Borrowed attention is the wrong trust mechanic for an ingested product, and the Assignment 1 plan names the blitz as a non-priority.
- No trend-jacking specialists. An audience expecting stunts will not accept a bedtime ritual from the same creator.
- No follower-count-first selection. A larger audience we never convert through trust costs more than a smaller audience we do convert.
