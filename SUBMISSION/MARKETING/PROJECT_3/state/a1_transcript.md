## Slide 2 — Brand Overview & Business Goal
- What We Offer & Why It's Different: A simple, drug-free wind-down ritual in a glass — one sachet, warm water, lights-low. Urban professionals 25–40 finish work at 9–10 pm, still wired — they scroll, snack, and sleep badly. Melatonin feels medical; chamomile tea feels weak.
- Melatonin-Free Formula: Ingredient-forward, built as a ritual, not a pill — magnesium + L-theanine + chamomile.
- Subscription Refill Rhythm: Designed to become a repeatable evening habit, not a one-off purchase.
- Taste & Texture for Work-Nights: Unsweet, light — designed for a work-night reality, not a wellness spa.
- Competitive Landscape: OTC sleep gummies and melatonin tablets; herbal/sleep teas; wellness apps (guided wind-downs); the default alternative — doing nothing and scrolling.
- Strategic stance: We compete with the habit of not switching off, not with pharmacies.
- Primary Business Goal: 1,000 Paying Subscribed Households — target within the first 12 months of launch across Mumbai + Bengaluru. This is a business outcome — a recurring-revenue customer base — not a marketing activity. Marketing's job is the measurable path that gets the business there. Why this goal: A D2C subscription business compounds on retention. A loyal base funds every later stage of growth. The 1,000-household base is the foundation, not the ceiling.

## Slide 3 — Marketing Objectives (SMART)
- Intro: Two numbered, dated, and owned objectives — both traceable directly to the 1,000-subscriber business goal. The arithmetic closes by construction.
- 1 Acquire — Trial & Conversion: Ramp to a sustained run-rate of ≥600 trial-kit orders/month by month 6, converting ≥12% of triallists to subscription within 30 days of trial start — delivering ≥90 new subscribers/month at run-rate, including a ≥15% referral share. (Acquisition objective — owns the top of the funnel and the first conversion gate.)
- 2 Retain — Revenue Quality: By month 12, at least 60% of monthly revenue comes from existing subscribers, with third-purchase repeat rate ≥ 45%. (Retention objective — owns the compounding engine that makes the 1,000-household base durable.)
- Metric tiles: ≥600 trial-kit orders/month by Month 6 · ≥12% trial-to-subscription conversion within 30 days · ≥90 new subscribers/month at run-rate · ≥15% referral share of new subscribers · 60% monthly revenue from existing subscribers by Month 12 · ≥45% third-purchase repeat rate.

Locations: Mumbai — Powai, Lower Parel, Andheri; Bengaluru — Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR. Income: ₹12L+ annual household income — already pays for convenience and self-care (food delivery, gym apps, streaming). ₹999 is an impulse-try. ₹1,899/mo competes with one dinner out.

Active 10 pm–1 am — the exact window Off Hours is designed to interrupt — and serve.
- Who Is Not Our Audience: Insomniacs seeking clinical treatment; price-first general supplement buyers; anyone outside Mumbai + Bengaluru in phase 1.

## Slide 6 — Digital Marketing Strategy
- Intro: We target over-wired professionals 25–40 in Mumbai and Bengaluru who do not believe they have a sleep problem — and we meet them at the moment they feel it. We want them to think "this is a simple ritual, made for my actual nights," and to do one thing: try the 10-night kit.
- Broad approach — rest-and-consolidation first: Everything we do digitally exists to convert a trial into a calm, repeatable evening habit and then into a subscription — not to chase reach.
- The Channels Work as One Loop, Not a Stack: Earn First Attention — short-video and search content earn honest attention and answer the ingredient questions that build trust. Convert at Low Friction — the storefront converts with a low-friction ₹999 trial — no commitment, no risk. Own the Relationship — pack insert, WhatsApp refill concierge, and the fortnightly sleep-journal letter carry the relationship through the 10-day judgement window and into subscription. Paid Amplifies, Never Leads — paid spend exists mainly to re-find people already in the loop — it follows proven messages, never precedes them.
- Phase Sequencing: Phase 1 — Launch: short-video, search, storefront, owned channels. Build the trial-to-subscription loop. Prove the retention engine. Phase 2 — Partnerships: brand partnerships — company wellness programmes, clinics — arrive once the subscriber base makes Off Hours worth partnering with.

## Slide 7 — PESO Media Mix
- Intro: Four media types working as one system — Earned and Shared create believable first contact; Owned converts and keeps; Paid only accelerates what the other three already prove. Spend follows pull, never precedes it.
- Paid: Retargeting to site visitors & video viewers; later, sponsored discovery inside quick-commerce listings once demand exists. Primary job: re-find warm audiences; amplifies proven messages only.
- Earned: Wellness editors & newsletters covering the "switch-off" culture; the brand's founding narrative — built melatonin-free for India's late-working cities. Primary job: third-party credibility the ingredient-sceptic trusts.
- Shared: Customers posting their night-routine rituals; 10-night challenge check-ins; referral links in group chats. Primary job: turns the ritual into social proof; peer recommendation.
- Owned: Storefront & product pages, WhatsApp refill concierge (opt-in), sleep-journal email letter, pack inserts. Primary job: the retention spine — where trials become subscribers.

- Table (Channel | Role | Why This Channel?):

  - Search (brand site + category articles) | Trust & research capture | Ingredient questions are the trust gate; the brand must answer them itself.
  - Own storefront + UPI checkout | Conversion | Low-friction ₹999 trial; owns the data the subscription model needs.
  - WhatsApp concierge (opt-in) | Retention & service | User-consented, India-native, high-open — perfect for refill timing and questions.
  - E-mail sleep-journal letter | Experience & advocacy | The 10-day judgement window needs a guide; letters out-sell loud campaigns.
- Named Non-Priorities — and Why: Television & Print — wrong age and wrong moment — unmeasurable against our conversion objectives. Mass Display / Broad Awareness Bursts — spend ahead of demand — awareness money lands before trial intent exists. Celebrity Influencer Blitzes — borrowed attention, wrong trust mechanic for an ingested product. Heavy Marketplace-Ads Dependence — a listing helps logistics, but we refuse to rent the brand's first impression.

## Slide 9 — Integrated Digital Journey — One Connected Loop
- Intro: Every channel hands the customer to the next with context attached — nothing is a dead end, and owned channels carry the relationship through the 10-day judgement window.
- Loop diagram: Discovery → Storefront → Onboarding → Retention → (back to Discovery).
- The Judgement Window: Days 1–10 are the make-or-break period. The sleep-journal letter and WhatsApp concierge run in parallel during this window — one guides the experience, the other answers questions in real time. Both feed into the subscription decision.
- The Referral Loop: Subscribers who share referral links in group chats re-enter the loop at Discovery — creating a peer-recommendation flywheel that reduces paid acquisition cost over time. The ≥15% referral share target is built into the acquisition objective.

## Slide 10 — Key Strategic Decisions & Brand Selection Test
- Four Decisions That Define the Strategy:
  1 Retention Before Reach — the entire system is sequenced to win subscribers, not impressions. Trial → judged experience → subscription is the value engine.
  2 An Understated, Proof-Led Launch — quiet, sharp, ritual content over launch-week hype. The brand earns its loud phase rather than starting with it.
  3 We Compete With the Habit of Not Switching Off — positioning against an evening behaviour — not against pharmacies or gummies — keeps the brand distinct and the message human.
  4 Partnerships and Mass Awareness Are Deliberately Delayed — alliances in phase two; awareness spend only after pull signals exist. Money and attention are spent in the order they convert.
- Brand Selection Test: What do you sell? — a melatonin-free night-drink sachet — a 10-night trial kit and a refill subscription. Who is it for? — urban professionals 25–40 in Mumbai & Bengaluru who can't switch off after late workdays. What problem does it solve? — evenings that never downshift: wired minds, junk-time scrolls, groggy mornings. Why would someone choose you? — a simple, non-medical ritual with honest ingredients, fair trial pricing, and support through the full first 10 nights — chosen once, kept as a habit.
