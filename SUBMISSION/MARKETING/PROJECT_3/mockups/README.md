# Visual executions (rough mock-ups)

The brief asks for **at least 2 ideas developed visually as rough mock-ups / storyboards**. These two
are deliberately rough design comps, not finished creative — they exist to show the *register* the
strategy calls for.

| File | Concept | Pillar | Platform | Why it looks like this |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `01_carousel_cover.png` | Concept 1 — "What's actually in your 4pm snack?" | Plain Teach | Instagram carousel | Calm editorial layout, one idea, no urgency device, plenty of empty space: the tone-of-voice rules from Slide 2 made visible. The sage accent is the only decoration. |
| `02_reel_keyframe.png` | Concept 4 — "Sixty seconds of nothing loud" | Show, Don't Shout | Instagram Reel | One continuous shot, natural window light, hands and label only, no face, no music swell, one line of text at the end. The timing margin along the bottom is the storyboard for beats 0–5s / 5–25s / 25–50s / 50–60s. |

## Before you submit

1. **Replace the product.** The packet in the Reel key frame is a stand-in. Re-shoot with your own
   product, and keep the rule the mock-up demonstrates: the hero shot is the *label*, not the logo.
2. **Replace the copy** on the carousel cover with your brand's version of "the thing people blame
   themselves for".
3. **Keep the restraint.** The single biggest risk in transferring these to a real design is adding a
   countdown, a badge, an arrow or a second accent colour. Each of those moves the piece toward the
   register the chart analysis marks as friction (`C006`).
