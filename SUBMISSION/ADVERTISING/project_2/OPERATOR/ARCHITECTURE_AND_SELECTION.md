### Working architecture

**A neglected consequence or responsibility → made visible by repurposing an existing medium → encountered where the audience can respond → one specific, low-friction action → dignified execution → comprehension and feasibility testing.**

This develops the earlier A/C routes: “the overlooked message” and “one message, encountered in context.” The safe-interruption route remains an alternative only if it clarifies rather than overwhelms. No product/service is introduced as the social solution.
