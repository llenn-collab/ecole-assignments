01 / NARROW THE CAUSE

The person
on the second seat.

Cause: pillion helmet use on everyday shared journeys.
Focus: adult driver-pillion pairs, stationary before departure.
Not a new product, app, rental scheme or service.

Age 18-30 and private-parking setting are strategic choices, not measured local prevalence.02 / 19
02 / NATIONAL CONTEXT

14,652

Passenger fatalities recorded in the non-wearing-of-helmet category in India, 2024.
54,122 total fatalities in that category.

MoRTH data via inspected OpenCity table records and Hindustan Times, 12 June 2026. Same underlying dataset; original ministry table cells not directly inspected. Not proof nonuse caused every crash.03 / 19
03 / THE BEHAVIOURAL OPPORTUNITY

Knowing is not
always doing.

346 adult crash patients in one eastern-India hospital sample.
96.6% agreed helmets were essential; 35.7% had used one.
Short-distance travel and forgetfulness were associated with nonuse.

Mishra et al., 2025; data March-December 2021. Hospital sample, not national/Mumbai prevalence. Association, not causation. PMC12020946 / DOI 10.4103/ijciis.ijciis_85_24.04 / 19
04 / AUDIENCE AND INSIGHT

Make the second
person present.

Audience: adult student / early-career driver-pillion pairs.
Hypothesis: on an ordinary shared ride, the second seat can become an afterthought.
Put the reminder where the second person is about to sit.

Creative hypothesis, not a participant quote. Local reasons for nonuse remain to be tested.05 / 19
05 / SINGLE-MINDED STRATEGY

Their own helmet.
Before the ride.

Proposition: the second person needs their own helmet too.
Action: both helmets on; both chinstraps fastened.
Tone: direct, considerate, no blame or simulated enforcement.

Correct helmet use reduces risk, not all risk. WHO road-traffic fact sheet, 20 July 2026; PIB helmet guidance, 12 July 2025.06 / 19

07 / THE SELECTED MEDIA INNOVATION

The seat supplies
the meaning.

An A6 card rests on the stationary pillion seat.
It addresses the passenger at the mounting decision.
No scan. No sign-up. No new service.
Facilitator removes it before anyone mounts.

Opt-in, permissioned, supervised placement only. No adhesive or vehicle alteration. Stop if safe collection cannot be assured.08 / 19

Where the second

10 / ONE VISUAL SYSTEM

Two seats.
Two helmets.

Equal-scale helmet symbols. One clear instruction.
Deep green + warm ivory + signal yellow-green.
Supporting poster reinforces the pre-ride check.

No certification marks, police styling, sponsor logo, crash imagery or claim of guaranteed protection.11 / 19
11 / DIGITAL EXTENSION

Who's on your
second seat?

A captioned 15-second illustrated sequence.
One bike / two people / two helmets / both fastened.
Check before the ride, never while riding.

HTML motion prototype and vertical artwork supplied. Not live-action footage or a claimed platform deployment.12 / 19

Small enough

14 / VALIDATION, NOT INVENTED RESULTS

Did the message
actually arrive?

Proposed comprehension test: 12 consenting adults.
Proposed revision gate: 10 identify passenger helmet use + fastening before departure.
Ask openly about meaning, tone, timing and confusion.

No tests conducted. Threshold is an operational design gate, not a validated benchmark. Any safety concern triggers revision.15 / 19
15 / EVALUATION AND LIMITS

Measure the check.
Do not promise lives saved.

Optional pilot: aggregate observations of paired departures.
Record both helmets, visible straps and unknown observations.
No plates, faces, names or equipment-certification claims.
An uncontrolled small pilot cannot establish causality.

Plan only. Do not report invented lifts, audience quotes, crash reductions or long-term effectiveness.16 / 19
