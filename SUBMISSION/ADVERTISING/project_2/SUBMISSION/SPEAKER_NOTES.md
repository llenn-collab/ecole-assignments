## Slide 4: Knowing is not always doing.

346 adult crash patients in one eastern-India hospital sample.
96.6% agreed helmets were essential; 35.7% had used one.
Short-distance travel and forgetfulness were associated with nonuse.

**Qualification / delivery note:** Mishra et al., 2025; data March-December 2021. Hospital sample, not national/Mumbai prevalence. Association, not causation. PMC12020946 / DOI 10.4103/ijciis.ijciis_85_24.
