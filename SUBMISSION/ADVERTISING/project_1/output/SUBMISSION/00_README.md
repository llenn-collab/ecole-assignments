## The campaign in sixty seconds

**State:** Meghalaya
**Niche:** the living root bridges — architecture that is grown, not built, over fifteen to thirty years
**Audience:** urban Indian knowledge workers, 28–42, whose professional output is obsolete within two years
**USP:** the only place on earth where the landmark you came to see is not finished, and where you can add to it
**Idea:** **The Hundred-Year Handshake** — someone reached forward across a century; you are the hand that reaches back, and then you reach forward yourself
**Line:** *Someone started it. You continue it.*

## Two things stated plainly up front

**1. No finished artwork.** Every visual in this submission is written art direction, specified precisely enough for a designer or director to execute without further briefing. The brief asks for a deck and for creatives specified across media types; it does not ask for produced assets.

**2. The research is secondary.** No field visit, no primary interviews, no verified figures from the state tourism department. Where a claim rests on inference rather than evidence, it is labelled as such in `02` §6 and carried into `09` §2 as a named weakness. Nothing in this submission is presented as verified when it is not.
