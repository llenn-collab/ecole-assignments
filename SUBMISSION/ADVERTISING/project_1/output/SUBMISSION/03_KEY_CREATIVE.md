# Key Creative — "The Second Hand"

**Format:** 90-second film (hero), with 30s and 6s cutdowns
**Deliverable:** D03 · Satisfies R07, R20
**Artwork status:** written direction only — no finished film or artwork is produced here

---

## 1. Why a film and not a print ad

The idea is about time passing. Print can imply time; film can *elapse* it. The single most persuasive thing this campaign can do is let a root visibly thicken while the hands on it change — and only moving image can perform that in one unbroken idea.

The print execution still exists, and it does a job the film cannot (see `04_TRADITIONAL_MEDIA.md`).

---

## 2. The one-sentence treatment

> One river, one root, one fixed camera position — and four generations of hands, ending with the viewer's.

---

## 3. Full shot list

| # | Duration | Shot | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Black. A single line of text: *"In Meghalaya, some bridges are not built. They are grown."* | River, distant, under everything |
| 2 | 0:08–0:20 | Close on a pair of young male hands, mud under the nails, guiding a thin pale fig root across a bamboo frame over a river gorge. The root is finger-thin. Fixed camera, locked off. | Hands working. Breath. No music |
| 3 | 0:20–0:32 | **Same frame, same river.** The hands are now older, heavier, scarred. The root is wrist-thick. The bamboo frame is gone. | Same river, slightly different season |
| 4 | 0:32–0:44 | **Same frame.** Different hands — a woman's, weathered, rings worn thin. The root is arm-thick and has fused with a second root. | Rain begins |
| 5 | 0:44–0:56 | **Same frame.** A child's hands rest on the root, not working, just touching. It is now a structure. Feet pass behind, out of focus — the crossing is in use. | Rain heavy. Footsteps |
| 6 | 0:56–1:08 | Pull back for the first time. Full crossing, in use, unremarkable, people walking it with shopping. It is infrastructure, not a monument. | Village sound |
| 7 | 1:08–1:20 | An elder's hands guide a **visitor's** hands onto a new, thin root at the start of a new crossing. The visitor's hands are hesitant. The elder's are certain. | Only the river |
| 8 | 1:20–1:26 | Hold on the two pairs of hands together on the young root. | Silence |
| 9 | 1:26–1:30 | Text, plain, centred: **"Someone started it. You continue it."** Then: *Meghalaya.* | River returns, then cut |

## 4. Script

There is no voiceover for the first 68 seconds. The only words are four title cards.

```
0:00   In Meghalaya, some bridges are not built.
       They are grown.

0:44   A root bridge takes fifteen to thirty years
       to carry its first person.

1:08   The person who starts one
       almost never crosses it.

1:26   Someone started it. You continue it.
       Meghalaya.
```

That is the entire copy. 44 words.

---

## 5. Art direction

**Camera.** Shots 2–5 are the same locked-off frame. This is non-negotiable — the entire idea depends on the frame not moving while everything inside it changes. Match-cut on the root's position in frame.

**Casting.** Real Khasi villagers, not actors. The hands must show actual working wear. The child in shot 5 should be visibly related to the woman in shot 4 if possible.

**Light.** Overcast, wet, high humidity. No golden hour. No sun flare. This is one of the wettest places on earth and the film should feel damp.

**Grade.** Desaturated greens, cool. Deliberately *not* the saturated travel-film look. The one warm element in the film is skin.

**Absolutely prohibited:**
- Drone shots
- Time-lapse of clouds
- Swelling orchestral score
- A voiceover describing how magical it is
- Any shot that frames a crossing as a view rather than as a working object

**Sound design.** Diegetic only, with one exception: a very low sustained tone entering at shot 7 and resolving at shot 9. The river is the score.

---

## 6. Cutdowns

**30-second cut:** shots 2, 4, 7, 9. Same locked frame, three generations instead of four. Loses the child.

**6-second bumper:** shot 7 only — elder's hands guiding visitor's hands onto the root — plus the end line. No setup, no explanation. Designed for the pre-roll position where the viewer will skip (see YouTube execution).

---

## 7. Production notes and duty of care

- Filming permission must be obtained from the relevant village *durbar*, not only from the state tourism department. These crossings are community property and living infrastructure.
- Villagers appearing in the film are paid at professional talent rates, not as "local colour."
- No crossing may be staged, altered or cleared for filming. If a shot requires damaging the site, the shot is cut.
- The visitor in shot 7 should be cast from the target audience, not a model — the hands must look like they normally use a keyboard.

---

## 8. Why this works against the grading criteria

| Criterion | How |
|---|---|
| Execution (G3) | One locked frame carrying eighty years is a genuinely original device, and it is inseparable from this subject — it could not be lifted onto another campaign |
| Strategy (G2) | The final shot converts the audience from viewer to participant, which is the campaign's entire strategic posture |
| Presentation (G4) | Restraint: 44 words, no music, no drone — the discipline is the craft |

**Craft standard.** This execution is the one place where finish and authority lead rather than participation. That is deliberate and is documented in `06_INTEGRATION_PROOF.md` §4. The key creative carries the campaign's quality bar; it does not carry its strategy.
