## 3. If this were produced — indicative production sequence

Included because several executions have lead times that a deck should acknowledge.

| Phase | Duration | Critical path item |
|---|---|---|
| Village partnership and permissions | 6–8 weeks | Village *durbar* consent — **blocks everything**, cannot be compressed |
| Film production | 4 weeks | Monsoon window; the locked-frame archive shots may need to be recreated rather than sourced |
| Outdoor build and planting | 8 weeks | Municipal permission for a living installation; horticultural contractor |
| Microsite build | 5 weeks | The annual-return email system, not the front end |
| Ambient installation | 3 weeks | Lobby landlord agreements |
| Instagram chain mechanic | 4 weeks | Camera template and compositing; platform review |

**Longest lead item:** village partnership. It is placed first deliberately — the campaign makes a participation promise, and that promise cannot be made before the community has agreed to it.

---

## 4. Launch sequence

The order matters, because the idea is cumulative.

1. **Outdoor first** (month 0). It needs twelve months to perform, so it must be planted before anything else runs. It also seeds curiosity with no explanation.
2. **Film** (month 1). Explains what the empty billboard is about.
3. **Print and YouTube** (month 1–2). Carry the two ends of the structure to reach and to depth.
4. **Ambient** (month 2). Converts awareness into physical participation.
5. **Microsite and Instagram** (month 2, continuous). The persistence layer; runs for the campaign's full life and beyond.
6. **Outdoor reveal** (month 12). The billboards complete. This is the second press moment, earned rather than bought.

## 5. Measurement

| Execution | Primary measure | Why this and not reach |
|---|---|---|
| Print | Spread readership; qualitative recall of the gutter device | The mechanic either registers or it does not |
| Outdoor | Repeat-passage recall at months 3, 6, 12; earned coverage at reveal | Growth over time is the asset |
| Ambient | Strands tied per site per week | Direct count of physical participation |
| Microsite | Completion rate of the 20-second hold; messages written; year-2 email open rate | Year-2 opens are the real test of whether the relationship persisted |
| YouTube | Deliberate: view-through is *not* the headline metric — brand recall among skippers is | The execution is designed to work on people who skip |
| Instagram | Chains started, median chain length, chains reaching 30 | Chain length measures transmission, not vanity reach |

**Campaign-level:** enquiries routed to village-council-run booking, capped deliberately. Success is not maximum visitors; it is the right number of the right visitors. Uncapped growth would damage the asset — see `09_RISK_REGISTER.md`.
