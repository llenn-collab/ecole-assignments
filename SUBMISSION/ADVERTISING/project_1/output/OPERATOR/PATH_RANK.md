**Why rank 2 was not discarded.** The P6 Open-door route scores well and is uncontradicted. It is therefore assigned the one job it wins outright: the hero execution, where the audience is necessarily passive and where finish is the requirement. This is recorded as a deliberate split of governance, not an unresolved tie. Documented user-facing in `06_INTEGRATION_PROOF.md` §4.

## 5. Elimination integrity

Two candidates were removed by hard constraint **before** scoring, not after. This ordering matters: a hard-constraint violation is disqualifying regardless of how well a candidate would have scored. Scoring an eliminated candidate and then discarding it would invite exactly the post-hoc rationalisation the method forbids.

Neither eliminated candidate has a score recorded anywhere in this run.
