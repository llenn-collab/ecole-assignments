# Solution-Architecture

How the chart reading became a campaign, without either side bending to fit the other.

## The governing decision

`SR-BEST-01` → **`BEST-LIFE-DOOR-NE-P8-WITH-LIUHE`**, score **0.8200**, requirement fit **1.00**.

Plain form: **lead with participation, not spectacle.** Build the campaign as an invitation the audience can accept and pass on, with every medium doing one job the other five cannot do.

The decisive factor is that Liu He supplies a **transmission mechanism** — something handed from one party to another and accepted. The brief's integration requirement rewards this heavily. The runner-up supplies authority and finish but no transmission.

## Why the idea had to be a structure

The brief's stated purpose is to distinguish integration from adaptation. That converts into a single design constraint:

> **A slogan can only be repeated at six sizes. A structure can be divided into parts, and each medium can take the part it is best at.**

*The Hundred-Year Handshake* is a structure with three parts — reaching back, the gap, reaching forward. That is the whole reason it was selected over more beautiful alternatives in [[Recommended-Answers]] and [[Path-Rank]].

## Distribution of the structure

| Part | Executions | Capability required |
|---|---|---|
| Reaching back | Print, YouTube | Present a completed past |
| The gap | Outdoor, Ambient | Elapse real time |
| Reaching forward | Microsite, Instagram | Accept and transmit a contribution |
| All three at once | Key creative | Hold the whole shape passively |

## The deliberate split of governance

The runner-up route (`BEST-OPEN-DOOR-NW-P6-WITH-DING`, 0.7170, rfit 0.77) was **not discarded**. It governs the key creative's execution standard: craft, finish, authority.

Rationale: the key creative is the only execution the audience experiences passively. Its job is to earn belief. Every *other* execution converts the audience to participant. Letting the film also demand participation would make the campaign monotonous and would waste the one medium that can simply be beautiful.

This is a judgement call, scored and recorded, not an oversight. Flagged operator-facing in `OPERATOR/COMMENTS.md` §5.1 and user-facing in `06_INTEGRATION_PROOF.md` §4. It also converts [[Hidden-Problems]] HID-04 from a threat into an assignment — the seductive route is given the one job where seduction is correct.

## Elimination before scoring

Two candidates were removed by hard constraint **before** any score was computed:

| Candidate | Violated |
|---|---|
| `BEST-RIDE-THE-HORSE-AT-P2` | R05 — collapses into conventional destination advertising |
| `BEST-PUSH-THE-SHOWPIECE-AT-P9` | R05 + R01 — amplifying the showpiece is the adapt-campaign failure mode itself |

Neither carries a recorded score anywhere in this run. Scoring an eliminated candidate and then discarding it would invite post-hoc rationalisation.

## Direction of influence

`SUP-001`: live requirement fit **widened** the P8/P6 margin but **reordered nothing**, in any slot.

This is the correct result. Had the brief reordered a slot, it would mean the assignment was driving the chart reading rather than being informed by it. The brief sharpened confidence; it did not manufacture an answer.
