# Integration Proof & Media Rationale

**Deliverable:** D06 · Satisfies R01, R10, R22, R18

The brief's stated purpose is *"Learning the difference between a truly integrated campaign and an adapt campaign."* This document submits the campaign to that test formally, rather than asserting it passed.

---

## 1. The test

An **adapt campaign** takes one execution and resizes it. Evidence of an adapt: you can describe the campaign once and then say "and the same thing, but square."

An **integrated campaign** gives each medium a job that relies on something that medium can do and the others cannot. Evidence of integration: **removing one execution removes a capability, not just a placement.**

The formal test applied below:

> **The Swap Test.** For each pair of executions, ask: could execution A be run in execution B's medium without loss? If yes for any pair, the campaign is an adapt at that point.

---

## 2. The Swap Test, run on all six

| Execution | Could it run in another medium? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Print — "The Gap"** | No. The mechanic is the physical magazine gutter and the reader flattening it by hand. Screens have no fold. | Cannot be swapped |
| **Outdoor — growing billboard** | No. Requires a fixed location observed repeatedly by the same person over twelve months. No other channel has persistence-in-place. | Cannot be swapped |
| **Ambient — lobby rope** | No. Requires the audience's hands and their physical daily route. No screen medium can take a braided fibre contribution. | Cannot be swapped |
| **Microsite — hold, write, receive** | No. Requires state: it stores an individual's message and returns it a year later with a stranger's attached. Broadcast media cannot remember an individual. | Cannot be swapped |
| **YouTube — the skip button** | No. Depends on a skip control that exists only in skippable pre-roll. OTT, cinema and print have no skip. | Cannot be swapped |
| **Instagram — one-to-one chain** | No. Requires peer-to-peer transmission with identity attached. No broadcast medium can have the audience be the distribution. | Cannot be swapped |

**Result: 6/6 cannot be swapped.** No execution is a resize of another.

---

## 3. The Removal Test

What is actually lost if each is deleted?

| Remove | Capability lost |
|---|---|
| Print | The only execution where two moments in time are present *simultaneously* |
| Outdoor | The only execution that elapses real time in public |
| Ambient | The only execution the audience performs with their body |
| Microsite | The only execution that persists and reciprocates over years |
| YouTube | The only execution that uses the audience's impatience as the argument |
| Instagram | The only execution transmitted *between* audience members |

Every removal costs a distinct capability. None costs merely "a placement."

## 4. How one idea survives six shapes

The idea is a **structure**, not a slogan: a handshake has two ends and a gap between them.

| Part of the structure | Executions that carry it |
|---|---|
| **Reaching back** — receiving what someone began | Print (1998 → 2024), YouTube (Year 1 → Year 30) |
| **The gap** — the time in between | Outdoor (12 months of growth), Ambient (weeks of braiding) |
| **Reaching forward** — beginning something for a stranger | Microsite (message to a future visitor), Instagram (pass to one person) |
| **All three at once** | Key creative — "The Second Hand" |

This is why a slogan would have failed. "Incredible Meghalaya" can only be repeated at six sizes. A handshake can be **divided into its parts**, and each medium takes the part it is best at.

### The deliberate exception

The key creative is governed by a different standard from the rest of the campaign: **craft and authority lead, not participation.** That is intentional and it is the one place where the campaign's secondary strategic route is allowed to govern.

Rationale: the key creative's job is to set the quality bar and earn the right to be believed. It is the only execution the audience watches passively. Every *other* execution converts them to participant. Letting the film also demand participation would make the campaign monotonous and would waste the one medium that can simply be beautiful.

This is a documented, scored trade-off, not an oversight. See `OPERATOR/PATH_RANK.md` for the competing route that governs it and the margin by which it lost the primary position.

## 5. Media rationale — why these six and not others

| Chosen | Why | Rejected alternative | Why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Print | Physical two-moment surface | Radio | No visual gap; the idea is spatial |
| Outdoor | Persistence in place | Cinema | High impact but no repeat exposure to the same person |
| Ambient | Bodily participation | Direct mail | Reaches one person privately; loses collective visibility |
| Microsite | Memory and reciprocity | Google Search Ads | Intent-capture, not idea-delivery |
| YouTube | The skip mechanism | OTT | No skip button, so the central device is impossible |
| Instagram | Peer-to-peer with identity | Native advertorial | Passive read; audience is not the medium |

**Traditional: 3** (Print, Outdoor, Ambient) — satisfies R08.
**Digital: 3** (Microsite, YouTube, Instagram) — satisfies R09.

## 6. Compliance with the brief's exclusions

The brief rules out *"sight-seeing / shopping / common spots."*

| Check | Status |
|---|---|
| Is the subject a viewpoint or scenic spot? | No — it is a construction practice |
| Does any execution show the crossings as a view? | No — prohibited explicitly in `03_KEY_CREATIVE.md` §5 and in the campaign's rule 1 |
| Is shopping or retail involved? | No |
| Is this a "common spot"? | No — the niche is an intergenerational engineering method, unique to this region |

The campaign's core rule — *no aerial hero shots of the bridges as scenery* — exists specifically to hold this line. The moment the crossings become a view, the campaign becomes sightseeing and fails the brief.

## 7. Self-assessment against the grading criteria

| Criterion | Claim | Honest weakness |
|---|---|---|
| **Research** | Niche chosen on structural properties; competitive set mapped; six media chosen against named capabilities | Secondary research only; figures need verification with the state tourism department |
| **Strategy** | Audience defined by working condition; USP derives from the niche and nothing else could claim it | Audience insight is reasoned, not yet validated by primary interviews |
| **Execution** | Six executions, 6/6 pass the Swap Test, each carrying a named part of the idea's structure | The outdoor execution is operationally demanding and may need the mechanical fallback |
| **Presentation** | Structured deck, explicit requirement mapping, integration tested rather than asserted | No finished artwork — by design, and stated up front |

The weaknesses are listed because a campaign that hides them is harder to trust than one that names them. Each is carried through to `09_RISK_REGISTER.md` with a mitigation.
