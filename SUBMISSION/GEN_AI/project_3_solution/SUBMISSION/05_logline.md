# Logline

> **AURA Cold Extraction is the slow-pressed ritual for the space between arriving and beginning — cold brew kept cold, poured close, nothing added, nothing hurried.**

*30 words and under. Sentence case, no exclamation, no slogan punctuation.*

**What it carries**

- the campaign idea — pressure set down, not powered through
- the product fact — cold-extracted and kept cold, not heated then chilled
- the category gap — nothing added, which is the one promise the category cannot make

**Where it is used**

On the back panel above the parameter line; as the closing card of the 15-second film; as the campaign's social handle line. It is written to be readable in one breath and at three feet.
