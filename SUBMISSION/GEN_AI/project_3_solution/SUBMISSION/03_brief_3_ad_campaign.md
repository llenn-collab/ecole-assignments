# Brief 3 — Ad / Campaign Creative Brief

*AURA Cold Extraction — premium ready-to-drink coffee.*

### Campaign Concept (The Big Idea)

**Take It Slow.** The campaign shows one person choosing two unhurried minutes inside a fast morning: the cold case, the walk, the bench, the tab, the first sip.

The category sells the pause as a reward for hustle. We invert it: the pause is not earned, it is claimed. Nothing promises a better day; the day is not in charge of these two minutes.

> Research basis: Part 5 · Historical Evolution of Category Advertising; Part 7 · Q4; Part 7 · Q5.

### Hero Shot Direction

One frame, one subject, no face. A hand — linen cuff, one plain metal ring — holds the can at chest height; the other pulls the cuff back from the metal.

85 mm, f/2.0, camera at sternum height, focus locked on the frosted unprinted band and the grip. The can sits on the left third; the rest is poured concrete and, out of focus, the bright rectangle of a glass door.

Light: one diffused softbox at 45°, cool daylight balance, deep falloff — directional and tactile rather than the category's flat studio key. The frost band is the focal point, so the eye reads 'something cold is being held' before it reads the brand. No text needed.

> Research basis: Part 4 · Photography and Art Direction Codes; Part 4 · Material and Tactile Sensoria; Part 5 · Casting, Representation, and Human Energy; Part 5 · Film, Motion, and Sonic Branding.

### Stage / Environment

One enclosed set that reads as a real threshold, not a studio: poured concrete, an oak plank bench, a steel-framed glass door with morning light behind it. One practical source plus day balance, so the frame has one direction of light. Three props only: a small glass, a folded linen cloth, the four-pack tray.

For the cold-case asset the set gains the edge of a refrigerated bay — three shelves, misted doors, one frosted can among deliberately plain neighbours.

Explicitly not: an open field, rooftop, skyline, gym, office kitchen, or any interior that codes as wealth; aspirational signage reads as class signalling.

> Research basis: Part 4 · Photography and Art Direction Codes; Part 6 · Discovery Moments; Part 6 · Cultural Sensitivities.

### Models / Casting Direction

Hands and forearms only, one person: 30–40, creative or craft-adjacent, natural nails and skin texture, minimal make-up, one plain ring and one linen or knit cuff in frame.

Faces are excluded on purpose, not for budget: the subject is a two-minute act anyone can take, and a face turns it into a lifestyle portrait the viewer either matches or does not.

The rejected alternative: a calm figure in soft daylight is legitimate, but it puts the campaign in the same quiet-lifestyle genre as the category leaders, where our difference reads as one more pretty frame.

> Research basis: Part 5 · Casting, Representation, and Human Energy.

### Production Requirements

Path A is one studio day plus a pre-light half-day: a small build in a daylight-capable stage, not a location shoot.

Path B is a staged generation pipeline with a human gate at every pass that touches can geometry, label legibility or brand facts; the full steps are in the workflow file.

Proposed path: staged hybrid. Hero frame and back panel are photographed; supporting, texture and motion coverage are generated, with the same three gates in both paths. The campaign argues the pack can be trusted, so the frames where the pack is the subject are not left to a model.

**Path A — traditional production.** One studio day plus one pre-light half-day; the set is a small build in a daylight-capable stage, not a location shoot.
- Location: daylight-capable stage or a real threshold interior with a glass door; blackout available for the cold-case frame.
- Crew: photographer (analog-leaning, Comfortable with 85 mm and macro), 1 assistant, 1 digital tech, 1 stylist for texture and wardrobe, 1 drinks stylist for condensation and pour control.
- Talent: 1 hand model (option for 2), booking as an anonymous talent.
- Props: 3 spare cans per variant with matched batch codes, glassware set, linen and knit cuffs, oak bench, plain-neighbour cans for the case frame, lint-free cloths, glycerine-and-water condensation mix.
- Post: grain and halation pass, warm-neutral grade with deep blacks, dust and fingerprint retouch, condensation clean-up, no beauty retouching on hands beyond texture.
- Schedule: pre-pro 1 day · shoot 1.5 days · select 1 day · post 5 days · master delivery 2 days.

**Path B — AI-supported production.** A staged generation pipeline using the standardised image/video workflow from the course, with a human gate at every pass that touches can geometry, label legibility or brand facts.
- 1. Reference build (human): lock a private reference set — hero references from MB-006/MB-002, geometry references from real product photography shot on a phone against a neutral wall, and one annotated crop of the parameter line. AI is not used to invent the can.
- 2. Image generation (tool): text-to-image for the hero frame with locked camera, light and palette language; three seeds minimum; the human selects on one criterion — does the frost read at thumbnail size.
- 3. Composition and product pass (human-led, tool-assisted): composite the photographed can into the generated frame; relight and rebuild the frost band by hand; this pass exists because no generator reliably renders legible small type, a 204-profile rim or a believable dew pattern on a specific alloy.
- 4. Supporting and detail frames (tool + human select): generate the three lifestyle frames and the two texture frames; human art-directs, adjusting framing and shadow direction to match the hero.
- 5. Motion (tool + human edit): image-to-video for the 15 s take and the three cutdowns; the human edit sets the pacing, removes the generator's tendency to add movement where stillness is the point, and overlays the real product geometry where the frame gets close to the can.
- 6. Sound (human): record the tab, the hiss, the pour and the glass set-down; no generated audio in the master.
- 7. Colour and grain (tool + human): one grade to the same recipe as the traditional path, so the two paths are visually interchangeable.
- 8. Brand-consistency gate (human, non-delegable): a fixed three-point check on every asset — can geometry, frost band, label text — against the reference crop. Any asset that fails is regenerated, not patched.

**Where a human must intervene on the AI path (non-delegable)**
- Can geometry and rim profile (generators flatten or warp it).
- Small legible type: the temperature line, the caffeine figure and the batch code.
- Any claim on screen: the cold-chain window, the farm-gate price, the caffeine value.
- Final pacing and any frame in which the can is meant to look cold rather than metallic.

**Proposed path.** Staged hybrid: the traditional path for the hero frame and the back-panel detail; the AI path for supporting, texture and motion coverage, with the human gate applied at the same three points in both paths.

The campaign's whole argument is that the pack can be trusted — the research identifies an elegant can with bad liquid as the category's cardinal failure (Part 7 · Q5). So the assets in which the pack is the subject (hero, back panel) are photographed; the assets in which the pack is context can be generated, which keeps the schedule and budget realistic without staking the promise on a generator's ability to draw a rim.
