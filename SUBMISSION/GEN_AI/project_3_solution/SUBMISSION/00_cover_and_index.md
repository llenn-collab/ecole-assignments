## How to read the traces

Each decision in these briefs carries a *Research basis* line naming the Assignment-1 reference it comes from (part, question or table) and, where a visual choice rests on it, the board pin it points at. Nothing in this package is a preference without a trace: if a decision could not be traced, it was reworked or it is listed as a declared gap (see `08_risks_and_how_they_are_handled.md`, risk 8).
