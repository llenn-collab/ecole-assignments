# Production & AI Workflow

## The short version

Two frames carry the promise — the hero and the back-panel detail. They are **photographed**, not generated. Everything else can be produced with the standard image/video generation workflow under a human gate. That split is the whole production plan.

## Path A — traditional production (photographed)

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Location** | daylight-capable stage or a real threshold interior with a glass door; blackout available for the cold-case frame. |
| **Crew** | photographer (analog-leaning, Comfortable with 85 mm and macro), 1 assistant, 1 digital tech, 1 stylist for texture and wardrobe, 1 drinks stylist for condensation and pour control. |
| **Talent** | 1 hand model (option for 2), booking as an anonymous talent. |
| **Props** | 3 spare cans per variant with matched batch codes, glassware set, linen and knit cuffs, oak bench, plain-neighbour cans for the case frame, lint-free cloths, glycerine-and-water condensation mix. |
| **Post** | grain and halation pass, warm-neutral grade with deep blacks, dust and fingerprint retouch, condensation clean-up, no beauty retouching on hands beyond texture. |
| **Schedule** | pre-pro 1 day · shoot 1.5 days · select 1 day · post 5 days · master delivery 2 days. |

## Path B — AI-supported production

Workflow, in order, with the human gate at each step:

- 1. Reference build (human): lock a private reference set — hero references from MB-006/MB-002, geometry references from real product photography shot on a phone against a neutral wall, and one annotated crop of the parameter line. AI is not used to invent the can.
- 2. Image generation (tool): text-to-image for the hero frame with locked camera, light and palette language; three seeds minimum; the human selects on one criterion — does the frost read at thumbnail size.
- 3. Composition and product pass (human-led, tool-assisted): composite the photographed can into the generated frame; relight and rebuild the frost band by hand; this pass exists because no generator reliably renders legible small type, a 204-profile rim or a believable dew pattern on a specific alloy.
- 4. Supporting and detail frames (tool + human select): generate the three lifestyle frames and the two texture frames; human art-directs, adjusting framing and shadow direction to match the hero.
- 5. Motion (tool + human edit): image-to-video for the 15 s take and the three cutdowns; the human edit sets the pacing, removes the generator's tendency to add movement where stillness is the point, and overlays the real product geometry where the frame gets close to the can.
- 6. Sound (human): record the tab, the hiss, the pour and the glass set-down; no generated audio in the master.
- 7. Colour and grain (tool + human): one grade to the same recipe as the traditional path, so the two paths are visually interchangeable.
- 8. Brand-consistency gate (human, non-delegable): a fixed three-point check on every asset — can geometry, frost band, label text — against the reference crop. Any asset that fails is regenerated, not patched.

### The three human gates (non-delegable)

| Gate | Who | Why it cannot move to a model |
|---|---|---|
| G1 reference lock | human, non-delegable | the can's geometry and label text are brand facts, not prompts |
| G2 select | human | the frost must read at thumbnail size; generators cannot judge that |
| G3 brand-consistency | human, non-delegable | three-point check (geometry, frost band, label text); any failure regenerates the asset |

### What is generated, what is photographed

| Asset | Path | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero image | photographed | carries the promise; label legibility and alloy behaviour must be real |
| Back-panel detail | photographed | contains the facts the buyer is asked to trust |
| Supporting lifestyle ×3 | generated | context frames; no legible type in frame |
| Frost macro | photographed element composited | dew on a specific alloy is where generators fail most visibly |
| Pour detail | photographed | liquid behaviour is the product |
| Motion (15 s + 3 × 6 s) | generated coverage, human edit | pacing and stillness are the idea; generated motion must be cut by a human |
| Sound design | recorded | the tab, the hiss and the pour are the campaign's sonic identity |

## Proposed path

Staged hybrid: the traditional path for the hero frame and the back-panel detail; the AI path for supporting, texture and motion coverage, with the human gate applied at the same three points in both paths.

The campaign's whole argument is that the pack can be trusted — the research identifies an elegant can with bad liquid as the category's cardinal failure (Part 7 · Q5). So the assets in which the pack is the subject (hero, back panel) are photographed; the assets in which the pack is context can be generated, which keeps the schedule and budget realistic without staking the promise on a generator's ability to draw a rim.

## Constraints the production must respect

- No health, therapeutic or body claim appears in any asset (only what the pack prints).
- Cold-chain distribution is treated as a hard project constraint; if ambient is required, this campaign and this brand cannot be produced as specified (see risk 6).
- The regulatory facts the assets may show — validated cold chain in place of an ambient kill step, exact caffeine per can, no legal standard of identity for 'cold brew' — are taken from the research pack (Part 6 · Dos and Don'ts — Constraints & Sensitivities: Legal, Regulatory, and Food Science Boundaries).
