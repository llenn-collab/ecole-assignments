# Risks — the eight ways this could fail, and what we do about each

These are the risks a producer or strategist would want named before work starts. Each one carries the research behind it, the decision it constrains, and the action that keeps it from landing.

## Risk 01 — A single production pass can quietly break the brand's only promise

If the campaign is produced in one uninspected pass, the two assets that carry the promise (hero and back panel) are exactly the ones a generator is most likely to get wrong — warped can geometry, fake frost, illegible type. The failure would not look like an error; it would look like a beautiful ad for a product that does not exist.

- **Owner:** Brief 3 / production
- **Certainty:** med · **Standing:** watch
- **Research basis:** Part 1 · The Anxiety: Latent Consumer Tension; Part 7 · Q5
- **What we do:** Keep the photographed-hero rule and the three-point human gate (can geometry, frost band, label text) as non-delegable; any asset failing the gate is regenerated, never patched.

## Risk 02 — The two documents can tell two different stories

Brief 1 sells restraint and a two-minute ritual; Brief 2 sells a matte object. If Brief 2 opens with the object instead of the decision that shaped it, the package reads as a philosophy bolted to a can.

- **Owner:** Coherence / Brief 2
- **Certainty:** med · **Standing:** constrain
- **Research basis:** Part 7 · Q1; Part 7 · Q5
- **What we do:** Brief 2 opens with the same one-decision sentence as Brief 1 ('less print, more material'), and every packaging claim must be showable in one of the campaign's frames.

## Risk 03 — 'Ritual' is a spent word in this category

The word is already on half the category's packaging and the research flags unverifiable craft language as meaningless to modern buyers. A brand whose differentiator is honesty cannot open with a word it cannot define.

- **Owner:** Brief 1 / Brief 2
- **Certainty:** med · **Standing:** watch
- **Research basis:** Part 6 · Cultural Sensitivities; Part 7 · Q3; Part 7 · Q5
- **What we do:** Never use 'ritual' alone: every use carries a time or a temperature ('the two-minute ritual', 'steeped eighteen hours'), and the brand's own ritual is defined once, on the pack.

## Risk 04 — The category's default look will absorb the brand's restraint

Restraint is now the category's house style: Swiss grotesques, neutral tones, generous space. A brand differentiated only by tasteful restraint would be indistinguishable within a season, which is precisely the failure the research describes.

- **Owner:** Brief 2 / Brief 3
- **Certainty:** high · **Standing:** watch
- **Research basis:** Part 1 · The Threshold; Part 4 · Typography; Part 7 · Q2; Part 7 · Q3
- **What we do:** Treat the unprinted frost band and the printed parameter line as non-negotiables; reject any layout in which the band is covered, printed on, or reduced to a decoration.

## Risk 05 — $5.50 will be read as marketing unless the pack answers the question

The research's buyer monologue is explicit: at premium prices the shopper asks whether they are paying for coffee or for a campaign. Design cannot answer that; only printed facts can.

- **Owner:** Brief 2 / Brief 3
- **Certainty:** high · **Standing:** constrain
- **Research basis:** Part 3 · The Questions: The Silent Internal Monologue; Part 4 · Label Information Architecture; Part 7 · Q4
- **What we do:** Print the answers on the surface: extraction hours, holding temperature, exact caffeine, batch window, farm-gate price. If a fact cannot be verified, it does not appear.

## Risk 06 — Ambient distribution would silently cancel the product

The concept requires an unbroken cold chain. If the brand is later pushed into ambient shelf distribution, the coffee must be thermally treated, the difference disappears, and the packaging becomes the exact hypocrisy the brand was built against.

- **Owner:** Commercial / Brief 3
- **Certainty:** high · **Standing:** watch
- **Research basis:** Part 1 · The Anxiety: Latent Consumer Tension; Part 7 · Q1; Part 7 · Q5
- **What we do:** State cold chain as a project constraint in the brief, not a preference: no ambient endcap, no unrefrigerated transit, and no launch in a channel that cannot hold 38°F.

## Risk 07 — Quiet craft can read as class signalling

Restrained, expensive-looking minimalism photographed in designer interiors can read as gatekeeping — the research warns about both coffee elitism and cultural appropriation in premium coffee imagery.

- **Owner:** Brief 3 / campaign
- **Certainty:** med · **Standing:** watch
- **Research basis:** Part 6 · Cultural Sensitivities
- **What we do:** Keep the act in semi-public thresholds rather than private luxury interiors; show price and process rather than provenance poetry; educate with warmth, never with a tasting note the drinker cannot repeat.

## Risk 08 — The board and insight list the brief requires are not in the research pack

The brief requires every major decision to trace to a research finding, an insight, or a specific mood-board pin. The research pack contains the findings and the insight material, but no mood-board or pin content at all — so any pin reference in this package is a reconstruction from the pack's art-direction codes and must be replaced with the real board before submission.

- **Owner:** Operator / all briefs
- **Certainty:** high · **Standing:** watch
- **Research basis:** Part 4 · Packaging Formats and Semiotic Significance; Part 4 · Photography and Art Direction Codes; Part 4 · Material and Tactile Sensoria; Part 5 · Casting, Representation, and Human Energy
- **What we do:** Swap MB-001…MB-012 for the real Pinterest board titles and add the pin URLs; the referenced visual styles are unchanged, only their identifiers are provisional. This is the package's only declared, unavoidable input gap.

## The one that is not a risk but a gap

Risk 8 is different from the others: it is not a way the work could fail, it is an input that was not supplied (the live board). It is declared here because the brief is strict about tracing decisions, and declaring it is more honest than inventing pins. Nothing else in this package is waiting on it.
