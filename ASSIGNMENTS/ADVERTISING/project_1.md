ADVERTISING – LAB4VC

# STATE TOURISM: INTEGRATED ADVERTISING CAMPAIGN
Creating an integrated advertising campaign across 6 media types, where the big idea
is crafted to complement each media type, rather than simply making size adapts.

# PICK A STATE
Could be Indian / International
Your target audience can be from the host country or a foreign land

# IDENTIFY YOUR STATE’S TOURISM NICHE / OPPORTUNITY
Tourism can explore nuances like local traditions and cultures, cuisine, history,
archeology, adventure, underground music scene, etc. Avoid picking activities like
sight-seeing / shopping / common spots.

# CREATE
### 1. Your Big Campaign Idea: This should be backed by research, target audience
understanding, and USP of your State’s tourism identiﬁed through research

### 2. One Key Creative: This can be a print ad or a video ad; it will become the creative
that represents your big campaign idea.

### 3. Full Campaign Creatives that include:
Traditional Media: Ads in any 3 channels
(Print / Outdoor / Ambient / Direct Marketing / Event / any other)

Digital Media: Ads in any 3 channels
(Website, Microsite, Social, YouTube, Native Channels, Google Ads, OTT, any other)

#### Important: Craft your campaign idea to suit each media type.

# Final Deliverable: State Tourism Ad Campaign Deck
Includes: USP | Target Group | Creative Strategy
Key Creative + Creatives across media types

# Project Timelines

### Week 01 - 24 Aug:
Present Chosen State + USP & TG + Minimum 3 Creative Routes
(Elevator Pitch Format)

### Week 02 - 2nd Sep: Final Creative Route + 1 Key Creative Completed + Shortlist of
Media you will be using for your campaign

### Week 03 - 9th Sep: WIP - Campaign Creatives Designed

### Week 04 - 16th Sep: NLET Submission

# Grading Criteria
Research: Understanding of media types available and how to use them
Strategy: Insight on brand and target group
Execution: Creativity of the big idea, and how it’s carried across media types
Presentation: Final output

# Project Purpose
Learning the difference between a truly integrated campaign and an adapt campaign. When an
integrated campaign presents the big idea in different media by using that particular media’s
strengths, it creates a better recall experience, compared to an adapt campaign.
