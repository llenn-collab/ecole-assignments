PERSONAL BRANDING & PORTFOLIO
BRIEF GIVEN: 18 AUG

PROJECT 01
YOU ON A SHELF: BUILDING A CREATIVE PERSONA

If you were a product, what would you be?
Crafting a Name + Logo Identity + Product + Packaging

Objective: Creating a memorable and relatable metaphor to represent you as a creative talent,
and showcase the value you bring to a company, while keeping it fun.

Week 1: Conceptualise
1. USP: Unique benefit you bring to an organisation: Functional | Emotional
2. AUDIENCE: Challenge in your employer’s life + Your role in their life
3. PRODUCT: Name + Logo design sketches

(Note: As part of process, you can do a SWOT analysis + use symbolism by defining yourself as a
colour / element in nature / school stationery supply. These need not be included in your
presentation.)

Week 2: Create
Complete your logo design and final copy for your packaging.
WIP packaging design.

Final Deliverable:
Product Name + Identity | Packaging | Mockups

Project Timelines
25 Aug: Concept presentation & feedback | 3 mins
01 Sep: In-class Presentation & Feedback | 3 mins
07 Sep: NLET submission with revisions

Graded on:
Uniqueness: Creative ideation and connection with self.
Use of Metaphor: Going beyond the common & predictable connections.
Design & Copy: Use of branding principles, persona & tonality.

Big Picture: Use this for any of the following:
Setting the tonality in your portfolio, especially when you write about your work.
Use this as an About Me page representation. Unfolding elements from this persona on every
page of portfolio, to keep it fun.
